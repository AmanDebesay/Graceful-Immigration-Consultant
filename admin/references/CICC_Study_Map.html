Annual Fees & Payments

RCIC Fee $1,809.25/yr — single July 1 OR 4 instalments (Jul/Oct/Jan/Apr)
RISIA Fee $913/yr — single payment due July 1 only
New Licensee Billed pro-rated from date of licensing to next July 1
Late Fee $100 if not paid within 30 days of due date
Late Interest 2%/month (24%/yr) on any overdue amounts
Payment Plan Apply to Registrar in writing. Default if payment missed → 15 days to fix or suspended
Payment Methods Cheque / Online banking / Visa or Mastercard
All fees Payable in Canadian dollars + applicable taxes
🏦

Client Account Regulation

Requirement Every RCIC must maintain a Client Account at a Canadian financial institution
Deposit By All client money deposited within 14 calendar days of receipt
Two Models Model 1 All monies deposited to Client Account
Model 2 Only unearned/unbilled monies deposited. Must apply consistently all year.
Notify Council Within 15 days of opening or closing any Client Account
Reconciliation Monthly. Must be done within 90 days after each month end
Keep Records Reconciliation records kept for 6 years
Withdrawal Only for disbursements (with client permission), fees after invoice issued, refunds, or transfers to another RCIC/lawyer
Cheques Cannot be made payable to cash or bearer. Must be signed by a Licensee
Interest General account: interest goes to Licensee. Client-specific account: interest goes to Client
Exemption Can apply to Registrar annually (declaration) if you never hold client money
📁

Client File Management

Opening a File
When Open file at time of signing the Retainer Agreement
Must Contain Retainer Agreement, supporting docs, correspondence, billing docs, Client Property copies, authorization of designate, draft docs, applications
Closing a File
3 Conditions 1) All fees/refunds settled
2) Client Property returned to client
3) Client notified in writing that Retainer is terminated
Keep For Minimum 6 years after closing date
List Must maintain list of all Closed Files including date closed
Destruction Date Set a Projected File Destruction Date and record it in the file at time of closing
Destroying Files
Record Must Show Client's name + address, description of service, File Closing Date, Actual File Destruction Date
Confidentiality Maintain client confidentiality during destruction
Returning Property
Account For Account for client property within 14 days of request
Deliver Within 30 days of request

Retainer Agreement

When Provide within 10 calendar days of beginning any work
Language Must be in English or French, dated and signed by both Licensee and Client personally (not designate)
Must Include Parties, scope of services, fees, disbursements, payment terms, schedule, client obligations, dispute process, CICC contact, confidentiality, refund policy
Contingency NOT PERMITTED — cannot bill only if successful
All Fees Must be fair and reasonable
CICC Clause Must include clause: if client can't reach Licensee (death/incapacity), contact the Council
Changes Any changes must be mutually agreed in writing + initialled by both parties
Not Required When Licensee is salaried employee of an entity whose primary business is NOT immigration services
Withdrawal — MUST Withdraw If:
Mandatory Discharged by client / instructed to do something illegal / conflict of interest / not competent
Notify Gov't Notify government agencies within 10 days of withdrawal where Licensee's name appears as representative
Penalties for Breach
1st offence $250 fine + written warning (30 days to correct)
2nd+ offence $1,000 fine per incident

CPD — Continuing Professional Development

Hours Required 16 CPD hours per annual Term (fiscal year)
Mandatory At least 1 hour must relate to professionalism / Code of Professional Conduct
Deadline 11:59 PM local time on last day of fiscal year
New Licensees 1 CPD hour per month remaining in Term. If licensed in April/May/June = 0 required that Term
On Leave Still required, get 1-hr reduction per month on leave. Even full year leave ≠ 0 hours
Suspended Still must complete CPD. Must submit remedial plan before reinstatement
Carry Over Max 6 hours can carry over to next Term
Keep Records Retain proof of CPD (receipts, attendance) for 5 years
CPD Hour Multipliers
Teaching 1 hr = 3 CPD hrs (first delivery), 1 hr = 1 CPD hr (repeat). Max 25% of yearly total
Mentoring 1 hr = 3 CPD hrs (as mentor). No max. Being a mentee = 0 CPD hours
Writing 1 chapter/article = 3 CPD hrs. Max 50% from chapters. Book = no max
Tribunal Obs. 1 hr = 1 CPD hr. Max 25% of yearly total
Group Case 1 hr = 1 CPD hr. Must be 2+ RCICs, max 15 participants. Max 2 hrs/Term credited. Max 50% yearly
NOT Eligible for CPD
Excluded PME courses / Being a mentee / Marketing activities / Pro bono work / Same content different format (both can't count)
Penalties
Incomplete $150 fine + outstanding hours carry over to next Term
2 Consecutive Zero CPD 2 years in a row = Suspension. Need 25% of deficient hours + plan for remaining 75% before reinstatement

Professional Liability Insurance (PLI)

RCIC Mandatory enrollment in Council's PLI Group Plan. Even if employer has coverage or on leave — still mandatory
RISIA Must provide certificate from employer's PLI coverage — not the Council's plan
Policy Term July 1 → July 1 annually
Limit $1,000,000 per claim/per period
Aggregate $5,000,000 for entire member group annually
Deductible $500 CAD
New RCIC Must purchase within 30 days of becoming licensed. Fail → 30-day notice → suspension → 90-day → revocation
🏢

Business & Name Registration

Rule Cannot provide immigration services to public under an unregistered name
Business Name Must include words that inform public it relates to immigration/citizenship consulting
All Partners All partners in a Firm must be Licensees
Firm Business Articles must restrict Firm's business to immigration/citizenship consulting only
Changes Notify Council of any changes in writing to registration@college-ic.ca. Not effective until Council acknowledges by email
Name CANNOT Suggest:
Prohibited "Law office" / government connection / "the best" / "the only" / misleading number of partners / holding company / ethnic/racial endorsement
Penalties
Unregistered Name 1st: $500 | 2nd+: $1,000
Unregistered Firm 1st: $500 | 2nd+: $1,000
📬

Changes in Information

Deadline Notify Council within 15 calendar days of any effective change
How to Notify Email info@college-ic.ca — not effective until Council acknowledges by return email
What To Report Legal name, residential/business address, phone numbers, email addresses, client account details, agents list, authorized representative for absence
Penalties
1st offence Written warning + 30 days to fix
2nd+ offence $100 per incident → possible suspension/revocation

Bankruptcy & Insolvency

Self-Report Must notify Registrar in writing within 15 calendar days of becoming bankrupt, making creditor proposal, becoming insolvent, subject to garnishment, or business under receiving order
Restrictions Registrar may impose restrictions: (a) no Client Account, (b) supervised practice, or (c) must become employee of another RCIC
Penalties
1st offence Written warning + 30 days to fix
2nd+ offence $100 per incident

Compliance Audit

Audit Cycle Begins July 1 of each calendar year
New Licensee Submit all required docs on or before first anniversary of licensing
Exempt Medical leave licensees are exempt from Compliance Audit during approved leave
Selection Random or risk-based selection by Council
Fix Issues Must correct compliance issues within 30 calendar days of notice
Documents Must be in English or French. Translations must be by certified translator
Annual Requirement Must submit updated Background & Good Conduct Statutory Declaration annually
Financial Inspection
Process Inspector issues draft report → Licensee has 21 days to comment → Final report to Registrar
Late Submission $100 fine
⚖️

Good Character & Conduct

Requirement Demonstrate good character as a prerequisite to licensing AND as an ongoing obligation
Applicants Submit sworn statutory declaration with application
Licensees Complete prescribed declaration annually in Compliance Audit or at annual licence renewal
7 Elements of Good Character
Elements Fairness Honesty Integrity Moral/Ethical Strength Respect for others Respect for Rule of Law Accountability
Evidence Putting Character in Question Includes:
Examples Criminal charges/convictions, open complaints, found guilty of fraud/dishonesty, disobeyed court orders, suspended by other regulatory body, previous licensing refusal, currently bankrupt/insolvent, represented clients for fee without IRPA authorization

Leave of Absence

Types Family / Medical / Retirement (age 60+, 2+ yrs licensed) / Full-time studies / Working for Gov't / Unemployed
Duration Max 12 months at a time | Max 24 months in any 5-year period
Fee on Leave Family/Medical/Retirement/Student/Unemployed = $10/month
Gov't Leave 25% of annual fee per month
Suspended Cannot apply for leave while suspended
Still Required While on Leave:
Must Still Complete CPD hours / Maintain PLI / Complete Compliance Audit (except medical leave) / Notify clients / Maintain Client Account / Respond to conduct complaints
Title Can still use RCIC/RISIA title for biographical purposes only

RCIC Resignation

2-Step Process
Step 1: Intent Letter
→
Registrar responds in 7 days
→
Step 2: Acceptance by Council
Must Complete Before Resignation Accepted:
Requirements Pay all fees/fines / Acknowledge open complaints / Close/transfer all client files / Close Client Account(s) / Return Certificate of Licensing / Submit statutory declaration
Open Complaint Cannot resign In Good Standing if open complaint exists
Jurisdiction Council retains jurisdiction for 6 years after Effective Date
Re-Licensing
Within 1 Year Pay back fees, catch up CPD/PME, sign new Agreement — no EPE required
After 1 Year Must pass Entry-to-Practice Exam (EPE) again (unless waived by Registrar)
Not Good Standing Must pass EPE + pay all outstanding fees + complete/re-take PME

Class L3 Licence — IRB Practice

What It Is Licence to represent clients before the Immigration and Refugee Board (IRB) — a Restricted Area of Practice as of July 1, 2023
IRB Divisions ID Immigration Division RPD Refugee Protection Division IAD Immigration Appeal Division RAD Refugee Appeal Division
To Qualify Must be In Good Standing + complete an approved pathway + pass Specialization Exam
Education Pathway (5 Courses)
Courses Foundations of the IRB / ID / IAD / RPD / RAD
Full Program $1,000 (all 5 courses)
Exam Fee $450 per attempt
Max Attempts 4 attempts. After 4th failure → may need to re-enroll in courses before trying again
DNS = Fail Starting but not completing the exam = uses one attempt
Exemptions
Exempt Graduates of Queen's University GDP or Université de Montréal D.E.S.S. who passed Entry-to-Practice Exam get L3 automatically
Dual Licensee Paralegal/Lawyer can practice at IRB under their law licence. Must disclose to clients which licence they're using

Independent Complaints Review Officer (ICRO)

Purpose Reviews complaints that were closed without referral to Discipline Committee (closed on/after July 1, 2018)
Power Can refuse review / review & investigate / mediate / stop review / refer complaint back to Council
Scope Reviews fairness of procedures only — cannot review the actual merits of a complaint
Reports To Reports to Council every quarter with stats and trends
Appointment
Who Appoints Board of Directors appoints ICRO for 3-year terms (renewable)
Qualification Expertise in admin law + professional regulation. Cannot be a Licensee, current/former Council staff, or Board member
Removal Only by ⅔ Board resolution

Penalties Quick Reference — Standard Pattern + Exceptions

💡 Most regulations follow the same 3-step penalty pattern: (1) Written warning + 30 days to fix → (2) $100 per incident → (3) Fail to fix = suspension → revocation. Exceptions are highlighted below.
Regulation 1st Offence 2nd+ Offence Extreme
Annual Fees $100 late fee Interest 2%/month Suspension → Revocation
Bankruptcy Written warning (30 days) $100/incident Suspension → Revocation
Business Registration Written warning (30 days) $100/incident Suspension → Revocation
Using Unregistered Name ⚡ $500 fine + cease immediately $1,000 fine Suspension → Revocation
Changes in Info Written warning (30 days) $100/incident Suspension → Revocation
Client Account Written warning (30 days) $100/incident Suspension → Revocation
Client File Mgmt Written warning (30 days) $100/incident Suspension → Revocation
Compliance Audit $100 for late submission Suspension Revocation
CPD ⚡ $150 fine + carry over hours 2 consecutive zero years = Suspension Must complete 25% + submit plan for rest
Retainer Agreement ⚡ $250 fine + 30 days to fix $1,000 fine Suspension → Revocation
