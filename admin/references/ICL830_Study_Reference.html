⚠️ Key Point: Holding a TRV or eTA does NOT guarantee entry to Canada. The officer at the Port of Entry (POE) makes the final decision [A22, R179].
📄

TRV vs eTA — Who Needs What?

▼
General Rule (R7 & R179): ├── Temporary Resident Visa (TRV) │ └── for visa-required nationals └── Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) └── for visa-exempt nationals flying to/through Canada
TRV — What It Is
  • Issued by a visa office
  • Placed in the FN's passport
  • Proves FN met requirements for temporary entry
  • R7(1): Cannot enter Canada on temporary basis without it
Who is EXEMPT from TRV? – R190
  • R190(1) — By nationality (US, UK, etc.)
  • R190(2) — By documents held
  • R190(2.1) — Certain Romanian passport holders
  • R190(3) — By purpose of entry
eTA – R7.1
  • R7.1(1): Visa-exempt FNs travelling by air to/through Canada need eTA
  • R7.1(2)/(3): Some are also exempt from eTA
R180 – Continuing Obligation
  • (a) Must have met TRV requirements at time issued
  • (b) Must continue to meet requirements on entry
✅

Dual Intent – A22(2)

▼
Dual intent = FN who has applied (or may apply) for permanent residence, but also wants to enter Canada temporarily as a visitor, student, or worker.
⚠️ Intent to become a PR does NOT automatically prevent someone from entering as a temporary resident. But they still must meet TR requirements.

Applicant Must Demonstrate

  • They will leave Canada after their authorized stay
  • To what extent they have retained ties in their home country
  • What their plan is if their PR application is refused

Designated Learning Institutions (DLI) & Study Conditions

▼
DLI: A school approved by a provincial/territorial government to host international students. Not all DLIs make students eligible for PGWP — always check the specific list.

Study Permit Conditions — R220.1(1) — Students SHALL:

  • Enrol at a DLI
  • Remain at a DLI until they complete their studies
  • Actively pursue their course or program of studies

Changing DLIs — New Rules (Nov 8, 2024)

Must now apply to extend the study permit (not just notify IRCC online). Relevant: R215.1, R216(1)(e), R217.1, R219(1), R222(1)

Off-Campus Work — R186(v)(iii)

Effective Nov 8, 2024: Maximum 24 hours per week off-campus while studying.

A personal narrative in the student's own voice that explains the choice of program, how it benefits their goals, their purpose of studies, their intentions when they return home, and links facts to supporting evidence.

LMIA & LMIA-Based Work Permits

TFWP, ESDC assessment, genuineness factors, wage streams, and employer obligations

What is an LMIA? — The Big Picture

▼
An LMIA (Labour Market Impact Assessment) is a document a Canadian employer may need before hiring a foreign national. The government is asking: "Is there really no Canadian or PR who can do this job?"
Who Applies?
  • The employer applies to ESDC
Who Issues It?
  • ESDC assesses and issues the LMIA
What Happens Next?
  • Positive/neutral LMIA → FN applies for WP from IRCC/CBSA

Assessing Genuineness — R200(5) — 4 Key Factors

▼
FactorKey Questions
1. Actively EngagedDoes the business legally exist? Operating? Can provide stable employment?
2. Reasonable NeedIs the job reasonable for this type of business? Can this occupation be expected in this sector?
3. Can Fulfil TermsCan the employer deliver on hours, wages, benefits, and working conditions?

Wage Concepts & High/Low Wage Streams

▼
Prevailing Provincial Wage
  • Wage rate for a specific occupation in a specific region
Median Wage on Job Bank
  • Midpoint wage — threshold between high-wage and low-wage streams
StreamWage LevelKey Feature
High-wageAt or above provincial/territorial medianEmployer submits a transition plan
Low-wageBelow provincial/territorial medianAdditional restrictions (caps, etc.)
⚠️ Low-Wage Stream New Measures (Sept 26, 2024): Starting Sept 26, 2024, certain LMIA applications for low-wage positions face additional restrictions. Check ESDC operational guidelines.
💡 After a Positive LMIA: Employer gives LMIA confirmation number to FN → FN uses it to apply for a work permit from IRCC.
⚠️ Professional Question: As a consultant, you must be clear — do you represent the employer (LMIA) or the employee (WP)? Watch for conflicts of interest.

▼
The International Mobility Program (IMP) allows employers to hire foreign workers without an LMIA. These work permits serve Canada's broader economic and cultural interests.

Business Visitors — Key Concepts

▼
Simple Rule: A business visitor's economic home base stays outside Canada. They're not competing with Canadian workers.

Three General Criteria

  • No intent to enter the Canadian labour market
  • Activity must be international in scope: primary remuneration outside Canada; principal place of business outside Canada; accrual of profits outside Canada
  • Employer must be foreign
⚠️ Business visitor categories exist under CUSMA and Canada-Korea FTA (among others). No work permit required.

Significant Benefit — R205(a) & C10

▼
Purpose: To create or maintain significant social, cultural, or economic benefits or opportunities for Canadian citizens, Indigenous people registered under the Indian Act, or permanent residents.
Types of "Significant" Benefit
  • Economic: General economic support, advancement of industry
  • Social: Increased health and well-being
  • Cultural: Increased tolerance, knowledge, connections to similar culture
Five Key Officer Questions
  • Can the employer get an LMIA? If yes, why not?
  • What are the social/cultural benefits — really "significant"?
  • Is the applicant a leader or have particular expertise?
  • Is there sufficient objective evidence?
  • Am I convinced? (Overall assessment)

Key Supporting Documents for C10

Document Type
Education, certification, or membership documents
Proof of experience (>10 years)
International awards, patents, or evidence of recognition
Evidence applicant has been a judge of others in the field
Evidence of scientific or scholarly contributions
Publications in academia or industry
Explanation why an LMIA was not filed or why it's not practical

Intra-Company Transferees (ICT) — R205(a) — C61, C62, C63

▼

5 General Requirements

#Requirement
1Employed by a multi-national company
2Seeking entry to work in a parent, subsidiary, branch, or affiliate
3Enterprise has a qualifying relationship with current enterprise
4Transferred to executive, senior managerial, or specialized knowledge position
5Employed continuously outside Canada for at least 1 year by the transferring company
Duration Limits
  • Executives & Senior Managers: max 7 years
  • Specialized Knowledge Workers: max 5 years
Wages
  • Must be consistent with prevailing wage to avoid wage suppression

What is "Specialized Knowledge"?

In simple terms: This person knows something very specific about how this particular company works — knowledge you can't easily find in Canada. Must show: high degree of proprietary knowledge, company-specific expertise, advanced expertise, significant and recent experience.

IMP — Open Work Permits (OWP)

R206–R208 humanitarian and Canadian interest categories, conditions, and validity
🔓

What is an Open Work Permit?

▼
An Open Work Permit (OWP) is issued under one of the LMIA exemptions and allows a foreign national to work without being tied to a specific employer.
Unrestricted OWP
  • Can work in any occupation and any location
  • (Medical conditions may still apply)
Restricted OWP
  • Restricts occupation (based on medical status per R185(b))
  • May restrict location depending on category
📋

Conditions on Work Permits — R183 & R185

▼
R183 — General Conditions (Automatic)
  • Imposed by operation of law
  • Apply regardless of what officer writes
  • E.g., must not work without authorization, must comply with conditions
R185 — Specific Conditions (By Officer)
  • (a) Period of authorized stay
  • (b) Type of work / employer / location of work / times and period of work

Validity Period — What Officers Consider

  • Passport validity
  • Expected duration of employment in the LMIA-exempt offer
  • Maximum time allowed by the particular program or agreement

How Does an FN Qualify for an OWP? — Key Assessment

Officer must determine which specific OWP category applies, then assess whether the FN meets the requirements of that category. The FN must demonstrate: the basis for the OWP, eligibility under the regulation, and that all general admissibility requirements are met.
