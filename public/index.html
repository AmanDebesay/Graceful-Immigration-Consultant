Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message or call (403) 915-4230.

How much does a consultation cost?

A 30-minute consultation is $30 and a 1-hour consultation is $60 (plus GST where applicable). You can book online and pay by phone.

Do I need to be in Lethbridge?

No. Consultations are held by phone or video, so we can help you anywhere in Canada or abroad.

Can you guarantee my application will be approved?

No licensed consultant can guarantee an outcome — decisions are made by IRCC. We promise honest advice and a carefully prepared, complete application.

What is an RCIC, and why does it matter?

A Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant is licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC). Only licensed representatives may charge for immigration advice, and they must follow a strict Code of Professional Conduct.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Tigrinya, Amharic and Arabic.

How do I share my documents securely?

After you hire us, we give you a secure way to share documents. Please do not send passports or personal documents by WhatsApp or regular email.
